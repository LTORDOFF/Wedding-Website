Travel & Accommodation

Nantwich is a lovely market town, and we recommend booking your accommodation early as hotels can fill up quickly!

By Train

The closest major station is Crewe (15 mins away). Nantwich also has its own smaller station just 5 minutes from Dorfold Hall.

Accommodation

Some local recommendations:

  • The Crown Hotel
  • Rookery Hall Hotel & Spa
  • Premier Inn Nantwich
  • There are also many good Airbnbs in the local area.

FAQs

What is the dress code?

The dress code is Wedding Attire (Men in suits and Women in dresses). For our evening guests, the dress code is Smart.

Are children invited?

We are only able to accommodate older children (10+). Thank you for understanding.

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence at our wedding is the greatest gift of all. However, if you wish to honor us with a gift, we would rather a financial contribution towards our honeymoon.
